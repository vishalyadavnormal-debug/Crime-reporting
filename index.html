Why Online Crime Reporting is Important?

Online Crime Reporting System in India is a digital initiative that allows citizens to report crimes easily from anywhere. It reduces the burden on police stations, saves time, and ensures that complaints are recorded safely. This system improves transparency, accountability, and helps law enforcement agencies take faster action. Citizens can track complaints and feel more secure while reporting sensitive crimes.
